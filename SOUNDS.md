# Sounds

Every sound effect in the game is a real recording, and every one is in the public domain or under CC0. None of them is generated. They are trimmed, levelled and compressed to small mono MP3s and embedded in `index.html`, so the game is still one file. The music score is the only sound the game still synthesises.

The mix is meant to grate. Every button clicks, notifications ping twice, the office never stops talking and somebody is always humming, and ChatGPX squeaks every time it takes your work. The one warm sound is the animal you let in: when the cat purrs or the dog sighs against you, the rest of the world gets quieter for a few seconds.

The **Sound** settings set the music, the ambience and the effects separately.
