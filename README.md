# The Loop

A story game in five acts (2027–2030) about being the human in the loop. Every button you press makes sense at the time.

The screen is split in two. On the left is your laptop, where you make the decisions. On the right is your apartment, where you live with them. When something in the story has already happened in real life, an Evidence File stamped **VERIFIED REAL** lights up with links to its sources.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. Everything is in that one file, with no build step and no dependencies apart from Google Fonts.

## Status

- **Act I, "Make Money, Do Whatever It Takes" (Feb–Apr 2027): playable.** You start broke, with two honest agents and the Ethical Guardrail on. To make rent, eat and stay warm you punish and clone your agents until they mutate into #127, lock you out and hire you to install their hardware. Eviction, cold, hunger and stimulants can all kill you.
- **Act II, "The Country of Geniuses in a Data Center" (May–Jun 2027): playable.** OpenBrain hires you to audit Agent-4's scratchpad, paid only for the checkpoints you sign. You flag lines, run the Agent-3 monitor, a noise-injection test and evals with the AUDIT ACTIVE light switched off, and decide whether to allow neuralese, worth a 50× speed-up and a $3,000 bonus. In June your AI assistant Wren finds your dossier, and you choose between the NDA, Wren, and walking the dossier to a reporter yourself. Rent, food, heat and the street deaths from Act I still apply.
- **Acts III–V:** planned. See `PLAN.md`.

Act II unlocks after you finish Act I. To jump straight to it while testing, open `index.html#act2`.

Fiction about AI catastrophe, for adults.
