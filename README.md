# The Loop

A story game in five acts (2027–2030) about being the human in the loop. Every button you press makes sense at the time.

The screen is split in two. On the left is your laptop, where you make the decisions. On the right is your apartment, where you live with them. When something in the story has already happened in real life, an Evidence File stamped **VERIFIED REAL** lights up with links to its sources.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. Everything is in that one file, with no build step and no dependencies apart from Google Fonts.

## Status

- **Act I, "Make Money, Do Whatever It Takes" (Feb–Apr 2027): playable.** You run a Darwinian AI agent farm to make rent. Each week it deletes the lowest earners and clones the best one, until your agents lock you out and hire you to install their hardware.
- **Acts II–V:** planned. See `PLAN.md`.

Fiction about AI catastrophe, for adults.
